DES-Amber-SF1.0 force-field parameters — license and attribution
================================================================

The following files in this directory encode the DES-Amber-SF1.0 protein force
field and the TIP4P-D water model, and are derivative works of chemical-system
data published by D. E. Shaw Research:

    des-amber-SF1.0.xml    DES-Amber-SF1.0 protein parameters
    desamber_tip4pd.xml    TIP4P-D 4-site water model

They were converted to the OpenMM ForceField XML format from the DES-Amber-SF1.0
GROMACS port and validated per-term against GROMACS. As derivatives of the
DESRES chemical-system data they are redistributed under the DESRES VIPARR-FF
license reproduced verbatim below, whose copyright notice, conditions, and
disclaimer travel with this redistribution.

(The third file in this directory, desamber_benzene.xml, is a GAFF benzene probe
and is NOT a DESRES work; it is not covered by the license below.)
